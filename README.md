## 💎 The Vision

**GoldCraft** is a premium, high-fidelity platform designed for the modern connoisseur of fine jewelry. By blending state-of-the-art **3D rendering** with **Generative AI**, we empower users to breathe life into their unique design visions with surgical precision. Whether you are tracking real-time gold market trends or sculpting a one-of-a-kind ring, GoldCraft offers an unparalleled experience in digital luxury.

---

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🎨 Interactive 3D Customizer
- **Real-Time Visualization**: Experience jewelry in high-fidelity 3D using `Three.js`.
- **Stone Customization**: Instantly swap gemstones (Emerald, Pearl, Sapphire, Ruby) with dynamic material updates.
- **Cinematic Motion**: Smooth, premium animations powered by `GSAP` for a truly immersive experience.

### 🤖 AI Design Studio
- **Prompt-to-Product**: Generate breathtaking jewelry designs from simple text descriptions.
- **Sketch Integration**: Upload your hand-drawn sketches and watch them transform into high-quality renders.
- **Powered by Puter.js**: Seamless integration with advanced AI models for rapid prototyping.

### 📈 Live Gold Market
- **Real-Time Prices**: Stay updated with the latest gold market valuations.
- **Investment Insights**: Track price trends to make informed acquisition decisions.

### 🔐 Secure & Personal
- **Clerk Integration**: Enterprise-grade authentication and user session management.
- **User Dashboard**: Manage your personalized design cart, design history, and credits.
- **Admin Command Center**: A dedicated oversight panel for managing user requests and orders.
